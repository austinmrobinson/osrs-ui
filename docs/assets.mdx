---
title: Assets
description: "Download every icon, frame and texture as SVG or pixel-perfect PNG at any size. Free for videos, thumbnails and overlays."
---

Every asset is a vector traced pixel for pixel from the game, so PNGs are exact at any integer scale: no blur and no stray colours. Choose a scale and format, then download one asset or the whole set as a zip. Click a frame or texture to open the composer, which builds it at any width and height. For example, a 1920×1080 stone background or a rivet-framed panel for an overlay.

<AssetBrowser />

Static copies are also served at stable URLs: `/assets/svg/<group>/<name>.svg` and `/assets/png@{1,2,4,8}x/<group>/<name>.png`, listed in [`/assets/manifest.json`](/assets/manifest.json).
